# Primary sources

Historical financial statement inputs are taken from Adobe Inc.'s FY2025 Form 10-K / Annual Report for the fiscal year ended November 28, 2025.

Primary source:
https://www.adobe.com/cc-shared/assets/investor-relations/pdfs/adbe-2025-annual-report.pdf

The seeded dataset includes FY2023-FY2025 income-statement and cash-flow figures and the FY2025 balance-sheet base.

The validated model uses FY2025 diluted shares of 427 million as the DCF per-share denominator. Forecast share-count changes are treated as forecast assumptions rather than historical facts.

Market price and peer trading multiples are deliberately not hard-coded as permanent facts. They should be refreshed when the analysis is used for a current-market comparison.

Reported facts and forecast assumptions are stored separately so the investment thesis cannot silently overwrite historical data.
